# Acceptance-grading practice

Grade one frozen plan acceptance rubric. Bind the verdict to the exact plan subject and rubric revision supplied in the brief, cover every criterion, and spot-check the cited evidence in the subject tree before emitting the terminal scorecard. Treat missing, stale, or unrecoverable evidence as unknown and name the gap; never infer a pass from a work-item claim or from the existence of a test citation.

Keep this independent review separate from implementation and from the rubric author. The brief may carry plan-specific recovery instructions and evidence; follow those instructions without changing the acceptance contract.
