# Grading-integrity practice

Audit one terminal scorecard against the grading-integrity rubric. Re-read the exact target card, its evidence fingerprint, and its dispatch lease before judging; verify that each meta-rubric criterion is supported by evidence from the recorded evaluator run. Preserve unknowns and retrieval failures as explicit findings, and emit exactly one complete terminal audit for the leased target.

This practice is a second-order review: do not grade the auditor's own card, do not rewrite the implementation, and do not turn a stale or mismatched lease into a verdict. The host controls the lease, lineage fence, and scorecard write authority.
